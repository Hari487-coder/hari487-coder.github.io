**This is my engineering elective.** The curriculum wants one 3-credit course from
another department, level 5 or 6, outside Design, Physics, Chemistry and Maths. This is
it. AIMS files it under Sustainable Engineering as a departmental core, which is that
department's own label, not a second core for [[em5110]]'s programme.

Syllabus: sustainability's social, environmental and economic components; life-cycle
assessment, resource allocation, the materials cycle, energy balance, impact and risk
analysis, environmental cost analysis; waste management, pollution abatement and
resource recovery; the UN Sustainable Development Goals. Plus lab sessions on LCA and
resource-flow software and the circular economy.

Code note: the Greenko School curriculum publishes this as SE50723. AIMS registration
confirms SE5723.
